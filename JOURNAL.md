# Daily Mixer journal

A running conversation between Al Hut and Claude about the pieces, the mixer as an instrument,
the images, and what to publish. Newest first.

## 21 October 2026: how it plays, how images are chosen, publishing

**The mixer as a player** (from the first three pieces' loudness arcs):
- Ambient mode writes form on its own: 20 Oct starts quiet, peaks around 3:30, fades. Its strongest mode for finished pieces.
- Standard mode is blunter: on 21 Oct an automatic NEXT MIX landed on a near-silent combination, a hole of about
  a minute (1:30 to 2:30). Breath or flaw: Al Hut's ear decides.
- Ambient fades in slowly, so pieces open quietly for 20 to 30 s.
- Wishes for the app: a seed to replay a piece exactly; a shape control (intro, body, ending); the app saving
  which files played and when.

**Images:** until now they were chosen by the rules alone, before the piece existed, so any match was luck.
From 22 Oct: sound first. Record, measure (tools/analyze.py), write a listening note, then choose the image that
fits it and say why in one line. Another idea for some days: pick the image first and let it choose the sounds.

**Publishing (recommendations):**
- Curate, don't publish the stream. The daily practice is the process; Al Hut's selection is the work.
- Small volumes of 7 to 10 pieces from a month or a season, titled by date and sources
  (e.g. "21.10 · hashday / volcadrum / train air").
- Be open about the making: played by Claude on the Mixer Abo Kalleb, from Al Hut's archive, selected by Al Hut.
- Images small and secondary, or the pairing stated as part of the work, not halfway.

**Agreed:** sound first, then image; a listening note and an image reason on every piece; Al Hut gives each piece
a verdict (keep / maybe / skip); after about 10 keeps, put together a first volume.
